Why pixels, not characters

A title of 60 “i” characters is much narrower than 60 “W” characters. Search results allot a fixed width — commonly measured at about 600 pixels for desktop titles and about 920 pixels for desktop descriptions — so two titles of identical length can truncate differently. This tool renders your text in the same typeface at the same size in a hidden canvas and measures it.

A practical title formula

  1. Put the primary keyword and the promise in the first ~40 characters.
  2. Use a separator (— or |) before your brand, or drop the brand on long titles.
  3. Prefer narrow words where you have a choice (“tips” over “WORKFLOWS”); avoid all caps.
  4. Keep each title unique across the site.

Meta descriptions

Search engines often rewrite descriptions to match the query, but a good one still gets used for its target queries. Aim for 140–158 characters, lead with the benefit, and include a concrete detail (a number, a time, a format). Full playbook: title and meta description length.
