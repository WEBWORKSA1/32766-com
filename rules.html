Official rules — The 160 Challenge

Effective November 19, 2026

NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A donation or purchase does not improve your chances of winning. This is a contest of skill; winners are chosen by judging, not by chance.

1. Organizer

The 160 Challenge (the “Contest”) is organized by 32766.com (the “Organizer”). Contact the Organizer through the contact page.

2. Contest periods

Each round runs for one calendar month, starting at 00:00:00 and ending at 23:59:59 U.S. Eastern Time on the last day of the month. The theme for each round is posted on the Contest page.

3. Eligibility

Open to individuals who are 18 years of age or older (or the age of majority in their place of residence, if higher) at the time of entry. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Employees and contractors of the Organizer, judges, and their immediate families and households are not eligible. Residents of jurisdictions where skill contests require registration, bonding or other formalities not completed by the Organizer for that round are not eligible to win; the Organizer may limit eligibility for a round by posting it on the Contest page.

4. How to enter

Submit one original entry of exactly 160 characters (counted as visible characters) through the form on the Contest page, with your name or pen name, email address and location. Limit one (1) entry per person per round. Entries generated substantially by AI tools without meaningful human authorship, plagiarized, offensive, defamatory, infringing or unlawful entries will be disqualified.

5. Judging

A panel selected by the Organizer judges eligible entries on: craft (40%), impact (30%), interpretation of the theme (20%) and precision of the 160-character form (10%). Ties are broken by the highest craft score, then by a judges' vote. Judges' decisions are final.

6. Prizes

One winner per round receives the prize posted on the Contest page for that round (by default, a USD $50 digital gift card or equivalent). Prize value and any additional sponsor prizes are confirmed on the Contest page before each round closes. Prizes are not transferable; no cash alternative except at the Organizer's discretion. If a prize becomes unavailable, the Organizer may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Winners are responsible for any taxes.

7. Winner notification

Potential winners are notified by email within 10 days after the round ends and must respond within 7 days. Residents of Canada must correctly answer, unaided, a time-limited mathematical skill-testing question before being declared a winner. A potential winner who doesn't respond, can't be verified or is ineligible forfeits the prize, and an alternate may be selected.

8. License to entries

You keep ownership of your entry. By entering, you grant the Organizer a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to display, publish and share your entry with your chosen name in connection with the Contest and the 32766.com website and channels, if you opted in to being featured or are a winner.

9. Privacy

Personal information is used only to administer the Contest, as described in our Privacy Policy. Winners' names (or pen names) and locations may be published.

10. General conditions

The Organizer may cancel, suspend or modify the Contest if fraud, technical failures or any other factor impairs its integrity, and is not responsible for lost, late or incomplete entries. This Contest is in no way sponsored, endorsed, administered by or associated with any social media platform. By entering, you agree to these rules and the Organizer's decisions.
