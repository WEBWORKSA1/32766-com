Social media character limits

Each network caps posts, captions, bios and comments differently — and counts differently. X weighs emoji and most CJK characters as two and every link as 23. LinkedIn and many schedulers count UTF-16 units, so bold “Unicode font” letters and emoji cost double. Instagram and TikTok show only the first line or two before “… more”, so the visible limit matters as much as the hard one.

Hard limits vs. visible limits

The hard limit is the most a platform accepts. The visible limit is how much shows in the feed before truncation — about 125 characters on Instagram, around 150 on LinkedIn, and roughly 100 on TikTok's feed. Your hook has to land inside the visible limit; the rest is for people who tapped “more”.

What happens when you go over

  • X: the post button disables; Premium users can post up to 25,000 characters but long posts are folded.
  • Instagram: captions over 2,200 characters or with more than 30 hashtags fail to publish.
  • LinkedIn: the composer stops accepting input at 3,000.
  • Schedulers: tools like Buffer validate before scheduling, often using UTF-16 counts.

Tips

  • Draft once at the strictest limit you post to (usually X at 280), then expand for longer networks.
  • Avoid “bold” Unicode letters — they double your count, break screen readers and hurt search.
  • Links on X always cost 23 characters, so shorteners don't save space there.

FAQ

Does Instagram count spaces and line breaks?

Yes. Every space and line break counts toward the 2,200-character caption limit.

Do hashtags count toward the character limit?

Yes, hashtags are part of the caption text. Instagram also caps posts at 30 hashtags.

What's the X character limit for Premium?

X Premium (and Basic) subscribers can post up to 25,000 characters; free accounts get 280.
