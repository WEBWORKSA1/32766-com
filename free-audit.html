How it works

  1. Answer five quick questions (about 60 seconds).
  2. We review what you send — nothing is changed on your systems.
  3. You get a written fix list with impact and effort for each item.
  4. Optional: a fixed-price quote if you'd like us to implement it.
Is it really free?

Yes. The audit is how we meet teams who later hire us for implementation. There's no obligation and no automatic follow-up sequence.

What do you need from me?

Usually a URL, a sample export or a few examples. Never send passwords, customer personal data or secrets — redacted samples are fine.
