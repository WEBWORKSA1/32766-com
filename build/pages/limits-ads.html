Advertising copy limits

Ad platforms enforce strict asset lengths. Google's responsive search ads allow up to 15 headlines of 30 characters and 4 descriptions of 90, with two 15-character display paths. In Google Ads, every character in double-width languages such as Chinese, Japanese and Korean counts as two. Social ad platforms mostly give recommendations: longer text is accepted but truncated behind “See more”.

Writing to 30 characters

Thirty characters is about four or five words. Use numbers instead of words (“3” not “three”), cut articles, and lean on strong verbs. Write a mix of lengths; shorter headlines make it more likely that all three show.

What happens when you go over

  • Google & Microsoft Ads: the asset is rejected in the editor — you can't save it.
  • Meta & LinkedIn: text is accepted but truncated in most placements; your hook must fit the recommended length.

FAQ

How many headlines can a responsive search ad have?

Up to 15 headlines (30 characters each) and up to 4 descriptions (90 characters each).

Do spaces count in Google Ads?

Yes, spaces and punctuation count toward the limit.

Why are my Japanese headlines rejected at 15 characters?

Google Ads counts each double-width character as two, so 15 Japanese characters equal the 30-character limit.
