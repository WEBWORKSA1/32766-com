Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Summary

  • Text you type into our tools stays in your browser. We never receive it.
  • We collect information only when you submit a form.
  • We use Google AdSense, which uses cookies to show and measure ads.

1. Text you enter into tools

All counting, splitting, cleaning and previewing happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your text is not transmitted to our servers. To survive a page refresh, the current text may be kept in your browser's session storage, which is cleared when you close the tab. Your theme and cookie choices are kept in local storage.

2. Information you send us

When you submit a form (audit request, contact, newsletter, contest entry, donation pledge, job application or sponsorship inquiry) we receive the fields you fill in, the page URL and the time. Forms are delivered to our private inbox through a third-party form-relay service (FormSubmit), which processes the submission to deliver it. We use this information only to respond, run the service you asked for, administer contests, and — if you opted in — send our newsletter. We don't sell personal information.

3. Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Where required (for example, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Google's consent tools are used to request consent for personalized ads. See our Cookie Policy.

4. Embedded videos

Videos load from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com) only after you click play. YouTube's privacy policy then applies.

5. Hosting and logs

The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operation. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

6. Retention

We keep form submissions as long as needed to handle your request, job applications up to 12 months, and newsletter subscriptions until you unsubscribe.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, Quebec's Law 25 or U.S. state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your information, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Contact us through the contact page and we'll respond within 30 days.

8. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and contests are limited to adults. We don't knowingly collect children's personal information.

9. Changes

We'll update the effective date above when this policy changes.
