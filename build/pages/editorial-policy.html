Editorial policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Sources

Each limit is taken from the platform's own documentation, help center, API reference or specification where one exists (for example RFCs for email and DNS, vendor documentation for databases, and platform help pages for social and ad networks). Where a platform doesn't publish a number, we use reputable industry references and label the value as a recommendation or display cut-off.

Labels

  • Hard limit: enforced; exceeding it fails, truncates or is rejected.
  • Recommended: a display cut-off or best practice, not enforced.
  • Default: a configurable setting.

We also record how each limit is counted (visible characters, UTF-16 units, UTF-8 bytes, weighted, tokens) because that's the source of most errors.

Verification and updates

The full library is reviewed regularly, and high-traffic limits are re-checked when platforms announce changes. Each hub shows its review date. Reader corrections are investigated within a week.

Guides

Guides are written for practical use, include working examples, and are checked by running the examples. We describe uncertainty plainly (estimates, approximations) and avoid hype.

Independence

Advertising (Google AdSense), sponsorships and paid services are separate from editorial decisions. Sponsored content is labeled, and sponsors never change limit data.

Corrections

Found an error? Tell us. Material corrections are noted on the affected page.
