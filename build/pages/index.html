Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Every count runs in your browser with JavaScript. Text is kept only in your browser tab's session storage so it survives a refresh, and it disappears when you close the tab.

Why is the site called 32766?

32,766 bytes is the largest single term Apache Lucene can index — the limit behind Elasticsearch's “immense term” error. It's one of software's most famous hard limits, and a good reminder that every system counts text somewhere. Full story.

Which count should I trust — characters, bytes or tokens?

The one the destination uses. Social apps mostly count visible characters (X uses a weighted count), databases and APIs count bytes, JavaScript and Excel count UTF-16 units, and AI models count tokens. The checker uses the right method for each limit automatically.

Are the AI token counts exact?

They're estimates (typically within ±15% for English prose). Exact counts depend on each model's tokenizer. Use them for budgeting and leave headroom.

How current are the limits?

The library was reviewed in November 2026 and is re-checked regularly. Platforms change limits without notice, so confirm anything business-critical in the official documentation. Spotted a change? Tell us.
