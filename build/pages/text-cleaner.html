What each fix does

  • Sentence case lowercases everything, then capitalizes the first letter of each sentence. Re-capitalize proper nouns afterward.
  • Title Case capitalizes major words and keeps short words like “of”, “and” and “the” lowercase (except at the start).
  • camelCase / snake_case / kebab-case are for variable names, file names and CSS classes.
  • URL slug produces a lowercase, hyphenated, accent-free slug capped at 75 characters.
  • Straighten quotes & dashes turns “ ” ‘ ’ – — … into plain ASCII. It saves bytes and keeps SMS in GSM-7.
  • Strip accents / non-ASCII converts “café” to “cafe” and drops anything outside basic ASCII. Use with care on names.

Every action can be undone (30 steps). Your text stays in your browser.
