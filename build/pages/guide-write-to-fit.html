12 ways to write to fit

When copy runs long, cutting words beats truncating them. These techniques reliably trim 10–30% while keeping — often sharpening — the meaning.

32766.com editorial · Updated November 19, 2026 · 7-minute read

The techniques

  1. Lead with the verb. “We are offering a discount of 20%” (33) → “Save 20%” (8).
  2. Use numerals. “three easy steps” (16) → “3 easy steps” (12).
  3. Cut filler words. really, very, just, actually, in order to (“to”), that (often).
  4. Prefer short synonyms. utilize → use, purchase → buy, approximately → about, additional → more.
  5. Swap phrases for words. “at this point in time” → “now”; “due to the fact that” → “because”.
  6. Drop the obvious subject. “You can download it free” → “Download free”.
  7. Replace “and” lists with symbols where acceptable. “&” or “+” in headlines and ads.
  8. One idea per line. If two benefits compete, pick the stronger and move the other to the description.
  9. Kill redundant pairs. “each and every”, “free gift”, “end result”.
  10. Use active voice. “Your order has been shipped by us” → “We shipped your order”.
  11. Mind invisible costs. Double spaces, trailing spaces, smart quotes (bytes and SMS encoding) and emoji (2 on X, UTF-16 and SMS).
  12. Write three lengths. A 30-, 90- and 280-character version covers ads, descriptions and posts.
