What people split text for

  • Threads. Turn a long post into a numbered X, Threads or Bluesky thread. Numbering reserves room for “ 12/34” so each part still fits.
  • SMS. Plan multipart messages at 153 GSM-7 characters per segment, or send separate messages.
  • Search indexes. Keep values under Lucene's 32,766-byte term limit by chunking in UTF-8 bytes.
  • Spreadsheets. Fit long text into Excel's 32,767-character cell limit across several cells.
  • AI retrieval (RAG). Create token-sized chunks with overlap for embeddings. JSONL export includes per-chunk counts.

Choosing a chunk size for RAG

Smaller chunks (200–500 tokens) retrieve precise facts; larger ones (800–1,500) keep more context but dilute similarity scores. Overlap of 10–20% stops sentences from being orphaned at boundaries. Start at 512 tokens with 64 overlap, measure answer quality on real questions, and adjust. Make sure each chunk fits your embedding model's input limit.

Tokens here are estimates. If you need exact boundaries for one specific model, leave a 10–15% safety margin.

FAQ

Does “keep words whole” ever break a word?

Only when a single word, URL or base64 blob is longer than the chunk itself. Then it's split by characters, never through the middle of an emoji or accented letter.

Is there a size limit?

No upload limit, since nothing is uploaded. Very large documents (several MB) may take a second or two in the browser. The preview shows the first 300 chunks; downloads include them all.
